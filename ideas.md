# Chosen Direction — Mumbai Market Canopy

## Design Movement

Contemporary Indian street-food editorial: the graphic confidence of hand-painted market signs combined with the refined restraint of premium food photography. The site channels the tempo of a Mumbai food lane rather than a generic global delivery app.

## Core Principles

1. **Freshness in motion:** angled bands, offset content blocks, and quick micro-interactions imply food moving from grill to doorstep.
2. **Local generosity:** rich crop photography, abundant ingredients, and generous negative space make every offer feel tangible and welcoming.
3. **Clarity under appetite:** bold hierarchy and quiet utility surfaces keep ordering simple even when the page feels energetic.
4. **Crafted contrast:** the sharp white RegionBites logo lives against warm dark surfaces while food is celebrated against pale spice-toned canvas.

## Color Philosophy

The foundation is **charcoal-black**, echoing the supplied logo background and providing a confident night-market canopy. It is warmed with paper-cream surfaces for readability, fired with ownable **Kashmiri Chili Red** for urgency and desire, and tempered by coriander green as the mark of fresh-made food. Gold is used sparingly as a celebratory signal, not as a decorative blanket.

## Layout Paradigm

The home page behaves like a food lane: a dark marquee at the top opens into a staggered series of menu stalls and kitchen notices. Instead of centered section stacks, content steps left and right, with a large cutout meal visual occupying the right side of the hero and menu groups moving through the page in a diagonal rhythm.

## Signature Elements

1. A **canopy stripe** — a diagonal red-and-cream band that appears in the hero, section labels, and cart state.
2. **Order tokens** — compact coin-like price and preparation-time chips used consistently across dishes.
3. **Ingredient linework** — faint lime, chili, and flame silhouettes tucked into backgrounds to suggest made-to-order preparation.

## Interaction Philosophy

Ordering should feel like choosing from a trusted counter: direct, tactile, and reassuring. Menu-card buttons add items immediately, counters respond with crisp scale feedback, and the cart gives a practical, unambiguous confirmation rather than a theatrical interruption.

## Animation

Elements enter from their nearest visual edge in short 180–260 ms motions using a snappy ease-out. Food images lift subtly on hover, action buttons compress to 97% on press, and cart indicators use a brief scale-and-fade confirmation. Decorative ingredient linework drifts almost imperceptibly only when reduced motion is not requested.

## Typography System

**DM Serif Display** handles expressive headlines and selected price moments, bringing an editorial food-magazine quality. **Manrope** supports all navigation, menu descriptions, and ordering controls with practical clarity. Headlines use compact, high-contrast line breaks; utility text is tracked slightly wider and kept in all caps only for small labels.

## Brand Essence

**RegionBites brings freshly made Mumbai comfort food from the grill to your doorstep, with a warmer, more local point of view than generic delivery platforms.**

**Personality:** spirited, dependable, generous.

## Brand Voice

Headlines are appetizing, concise, and certain. CTAs sound like a helpful counterperson—not a software interface—and microcopy makes preparation visible.

Example headline: **Hot from the pan. Headed your way.**

Example CTA: **Bring this home**

## Wordmark & Logo

The supplied chef-hat, fork, and spoon mark remains the primary brand asset, displayed as a clear white seal on dark surfaces. A simplified spoon-and-fork knot glyph will serve as the browser icon and compact interface marker. The full “REGIONBITES — BITE OF JOY” wordmark is never redrawn or replaced.

## Signature Brand Color

**Kashmiri Chili Red — #C93A2B**

## Style Decisions

- The red-and-cream canopy stripe is a recurring diagonal transition and ordering-state motif rather than a decorative divider.
- Menu categories, dish identifiers, prices, and preparation estimates are treated as related **counter tickets**, using sharp corners, punchy labels, and compact numeric cues.
- Food language highlights Mumbai comfort cues—pav, rumali rolls, chutneys, tandoor marks, kala masala, and the rhythm of a neighbourhood counter—while preserving premium photography and quick ordering clarity.
